# Submission Consistency Checklist

## Model identity

Selected model, baseline, component names, objective, constraints, solver/training procedure, and route decision agree across paper, code, and configuration.

## Data identity

Dataset hash/version, sheets/files, sample counts, exclusions, missing-value treatment, units, entity/group definitions, and time/date conventions agree.

## Evaluation identity

Split boundaries, folds/groups, metrics, model-selection rule, seed policy, ablation variants, sensitivity range, and robustness method agree.

## Result identity

Every quoted number and table maps to a final artifact locator. Figures use the final files and agree with tables, axes, legends, captions, and narrative. Rounding is explainable and does not change rankings or conclusions.

## Claim identity

The paper does not convert association, importance, or sensitivity into unsupported causality; weak or negative results are not hidden; limitations match diagnostics; conclusions directly answer the contest prompt.
